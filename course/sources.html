约定对照

  • 数组采用样本按行；单样本列向量公式需转置转换；PyTorch Linear 权重存储为 (out,in)。
  • 本站回归数据项用 MSE（均方误差）；CS229 入门半平方和目标比它大 n/2 倍，梯度与学习率需相应换算。
  • Ridge（岭回归） 的 SSE + alpha·||w||² 与本站 MSE + lambda·||w||² 在相同约定下须 alpha=n·lambda。
  • PCA（主成分分析） 的 n 与 n−1 影响特征值缩放；符号变化不影响同一投影轴。
  • Momentum（动量） 是否含 (1−beta)、Adam（自适应矩估计） 的 L2 与 AdamW 的衰减、卷积与互相关都需核对定义。

主线之外 · 有前置要求的进阶路线

Nonlinear statistical models

Prerequisites: W05, W08, W09

Read: ISLP Ch. 7; Ch. 8 boosting（提升法）

Compare splines or a generalized additive model with a linear baseline（基线） on identical folds and tuning budgets. Show effect curves and discuss unreliable extrapolation.

Produce: An effect-curve comparison and held-out error table.

Survival analysis and multiple testing

Prerequisites: W07, W09, W23 and probability

Read: ISLP Ch. 11 and Ch. 13

Study censoring（删失） before time-to-event modeling. Separately, distinguish family-wise error and false-discovery control before screening many hypotheses. Ordinary classification accuracy does not replace these concepts.

Produce: A censoring example or a simulated multiple-testing audit.

Probabilistic latent-variable models

Prerequisites: W07, W12, W13

Read: CS229 §11 EM and §13 ICA; ISLP Ch. 12

Compare Gaussian-mixture responsibilities with K-means assignments. Derive one E-step and M-step for a restricted model before studying more complex latent variables.

Produce: A likelihood（似然） trace, responsibility plot and assumption comparison.

Convexity and SVM duality（对偶性）

Prerequisites: W02, W05, W08, W11

Read: Convex Optimization Ch. 3–5; CS229 §6

Work through convexity, Lagrange multipliers, KKT conditions and the SVM dual. Connect every multiplier to its constraint and show how kernels enter inner products.

Produce: A small primal/dual calculation with feasibility checks.

Sequences, attention（注意力机制） and foundation models

Prerequisites: W15–20 plus a completed project

Read: D2L recurrent networks, attention, Transformers; CS229 §14

Start with sequence shapes, masking and one attention calculation. This is a new route, not a claim that the 24-week course covers large-model training.

Produce: A hand-checked attention example and a small sequence baseline.

Research replication（复核性重复实验）

Prerequisites: W21–24

Read: arXiv; Hugging Face Papers; AIcrowd; DrivenData

Select one paper or challenge. Extract its data, architecture, budget, preprocessing and metric. Document departures before running and include negative results.

Produce: A bounded replication report with matched and unmatched conditions.

Advanced data workflows

Prerequisites: W01–03 and a concrete dataset

Read: Handbook: hierarchical indexing, time series, visualization; NumPy and pandas

Choose a real need such as a time-indexed panel or many-to-one join. Verify semantics and supported APIs rather than collecting plotting recipes.

Produce: A data audit with invariants checked around every transformation.
