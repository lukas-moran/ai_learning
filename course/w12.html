Concepts and derivation

Subtract the training feature（特征） mean to form Xc. For a unit direction v, projected coordinates are Xc v and their sample variance is v-transpose C v, where C=Xc-transpose Xc/(n-1). Maximizing this quantity gives the leading covariance（协方差） eigenvector（特征向量）. Additional components maximize remaining variance while remaining orthogonal.

For a matrix V of retained directions, scores are Z=Xc V and reconstruction is Z V-transpose plus the training mean. Retaining more components cannot increase the optimal training squared reconstruction error. That does not guarantee better prediction, and high variance can come from nuisance effects or units.

SVD（奇异值分解） of centered X gives the same principal directions through its right singular vectors; eigenvalues equal squared singular values divided by n-1 under this convention. A component and its negative represent the same axis（轴）. Repeated eigenvalues allow rotations inside an equally valid principal subspace.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

PCA chooses directions without observing the response

Center each feature before seeking a direction through the cloud. For a unit vector v, the projected score of a centered observation x is x-transpose-v. Maximizing the average squared score over v asks for the direction with greatest variance. The unit-length restriction prevents increasing variance merely by scaling v. Lagrange multipliers turn the constrained optimization into an eigenvector equation for the covariance matrix.

After choosing the first direction, require the next direction to be orthogonal and maximize remaining variance. The resulting scores describe a rotated coordinate system. PCA（主成分分析） uses X alone, so the highest-variance directions need not be the most predictive of y. Low-variance features can contain crucial signal, while a high-variance nuisance can dominate the first component.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

Why maximum variance also minimizes reconstruction error

For centered x and unit v, the rank（秩）-one reconstruction is (x-transpose-v)*v. By orthogonality（正交性）, squared original length equals squared projected length plus squared residual（残差） length. Summing over observations makes total centered variation fixed. Maximizing the captured projected part therefore minimizes the residual part. This equivalence uses orthogonal Euclidean projection and the stated centering.

SVD writes the centered data matrix as U*S*V-transpose. Columns of V supply component directions; squared singular values divided by n-1 give sample covariance eigenvalues. Dividing by n changes eigenvalue（特征值） scale but not directions. Either sign of an eigenvector represents the same axis. Equal or nearly equal eigenvalues can make individual directions unstable even when their combined subspace is meaningful.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

The argument in mathematical notation

Projection and reconstruction split total energy

\lVert x\rVert^2=(x^\top v)^2+\lVert x-(x^\top v)v\rVert^2,\qquad \lVert v\rVert=1

For centered observations and orthogonal projection, maximizing captured squared scores minimizes reconstruction error.

详细讲解 · 第 2 学习日

Derive the principal direction using a constrained variance problem

Let C be the covariance matrix of centered observations. The average squared projected score for unit direction v is v-transpose-C-v. Form a Lagrangian v-transpose-C-v-lambda*(v-transpose-v-1). Differentiating with respect to v gives 2Cv-2lambda*v=0, so every stationary direction is an eigenvector. Multiplying by v-transpose shows the attained variance is lambda. The largest eigenvalue therefore gives the maximum.

For C=[[2,1],[1,2]], directions [1,1]/sqrt(2) and [1,-1]/sqrt(2) have eigenvalues 3 and 1. The first component captures 3/4 of total covariance trace. Both original features contribute to the high-variance direction; simply selecting the column with the largest marginal variance would not reproduce that projection because both marginal variances equal 2.

This derivation clarifies the connection between CS229's variance objective, MML's eigenvectors and the Handbook's SVD implementation. The numerical routine may return the negative of the displayed vector. Compare captured variance and projection matrices rather than treating that harmless sign as a defect.

详细讲解 · 第 2 学习日

Project four points and account for every unit of variance

  1. Use centered points [2,1], [2,-1], [-2,1], [-2,-1]. Their population covariance is diag(4,1). The first principal direction is [1,0].
  2. Projecting onto that direction gives scores [2,2,-2,-2] and reconstructions [2,0], [2,0], [-2,0], [-2,0].
  3. Every residual has squared length one. Mean reconstruction error is one, total mean squared centered length is five, and captured variance is four. Explained-variance ratio is 4/5=0.8.
  4. If both features are standardized first, covariance becomes the identity in this example. No single direction uniquely maximizes variance. Scaling changes the question PCA answers, rather than merely changing the display units.
详细讲解 · 第 3 学习日

Keep fitting, projection and reconstruction as separate operations

Store the training mean and component matrix. A new row is first centered using that training mean, then projected. Reconstruction maps scores back into feature coordinates and adds the same mean. Check orthonormality, score shapes and reconstruction error. Comparing component signs directly with a library is weaker than comparing projection matrices or reconstructed values.

详细讲解 · 第 3 学习日

Verify variance decomposition on the four-point example

points = [[2.,1.],[2.,-1.],[-2.,1.],[-2.,-1.]]
direction = [1.,0.]
scores = [sum(a*b for a,b in zip(x,direction)) for x in points]
reconstructed = [[s*a for a in direction] for s in scores]
total = sum(sum(a*a for a in x) for x in points)/len(points)
error = sum(sum((a-b)**2 for a,b in zip(x,r))
            for x,r in zip(points,reconstructed))/len(points)
captured = sum(s*s for s in scores)/len(scores)
assert total == 5 and error == 1 and captured == 4
assert total == error+captured
print({"explained_ratio":captured/total,"reconstruction_error":error})

详细讲解 · 第 4 学习日

Compare the geometric and predictive purposes of PCA

Create a target determined by the second coordinate of the four-point pattern. A one-component PCA representation captures 80% of feature variation but discards the coordinate that determines that target. Record the difference between explaining feature variance and predicting outcomes. This counterexample prevents a common overinterpretation of an impressive explained-variance percentage.

The Handbook supplies practical dimensionality-reduction demonstrations, CS229 provides the variance-maximization formulation, and MML develops projection and decomposition. ISLP adds the connection to downstream modeling and validation. Choose the number of components within training/validation boundaries if it is tuned for a prediction task.

详细讲解 · 第 5 学习日

Further practice with worked solutions

  1. Why do opposite signs of a component not imply disagreement?

    查看逐步解析

    Replacing v by -v reverses all scores. Multiplying the reversed scores by the reversed direction gives the same reconstruction and projection subspace.

  2. Does 95% explained variance guarantee high classification accuracy?

    查看逐步解析

    No. The remaining 5% may contain the target signal. PCA measures variation of X without using y; predictive performance requires its own evaluation.

Worked example

  1. Center two points [-1,-1] and [1,1], whose mean is already zero. Sample covariance is [[2,2],[2,2]].
  2. The first direction is [1,1]/sqrt(2), with eigenvalue 4. The orthogonal direction has eigenvalue 0.
  3. Scores are -sqrt(2) and sqrt(2); reconstruction from one component is exact.
  4. Flipping the direction flips both scores, so reconstruction stays identical. Compare reconstructions or subspaces rather than demanding identical eigenvector signs.

Comparing the sources

ISLP Ch. 12 emphasizes interpretation and scale choices. CS229 §12 derives variance-maximizing projection. The Handbook gives visual examples; MML explains the eigenvalue and SVD connection. These are complementary views of one objective.

Dividing covariance by n instead of n-1 rescales its eigenvalues, not its eigenvectors. Centering and standardizing are different operations; standardization（标准化） changes which geometry PCA considers important.

Implementation

Fit the mean on training data, use eigh for a symmetric covariance matrix and sort eigenpairs descending.

Implement transform and inverse_transform using stored training quantities. Compare with library PCA on the same preprocessing.

Check orthonormality, reconstruction error and explained-variance ratios. Avoid comparing component signs as an implementation test.

import numpy as np

def fit_pca(X,k):
    mu = X.mean(0); centered = X-mu
    cov = centered.T @ centered/(len(X)-1)
    values,vectors = np.linalg.eigh(cov)
    order = np.argsort(values)[::-1]
    return mu,vectors[:,order[:k]],values[order]

rng = np.random.default_rng(42)
x = rng.normal(size=100)
X = np.column_stack([x,2*x+rng.normal(0,.2,100)])
Xtr,Xv = X[:70],X[70:]
errors = []
for k in [1,2]:
    mu,V,values = fit_pca(Xtr,k)
    Z = (Xv-mu) @ V
    reconstruction = Z @ V.T + mu
    error = np.mean((Xv-reconstruction)**2); errors.append(error)
    print("k/eigenvalues/validation reconstruction MSE:",k,values,error)
    assert np.allclose(Z @ V.T, ((Xv-mu) @ -V) @ (-V).T)
assert errors[1] < 1e-20

One-page recap

PCA preserves variance under a chosen geometry; it does not automatically preserve predictive meaning.

principal component（主成分）: A direction chosen to explain maximal remaining variance under orthogonality constraints.

eigendecomposition（特征分解）: A decomposition into eigenvectors and their corresponding eigenvalues.

singular value decomposition（奇异值分解）: A factorization X=U S V-transpose into orthogonal directions and singular values.

K-means will group observations rather than choose a coordinate basis.
