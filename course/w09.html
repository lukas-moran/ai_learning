Concepts and derivation

A confusion matrix（混淆矩阵） counts outcomes under a chosen threshold. Precision conditions on predicted positives; recall conditions on actual positives. F1 is their harmonic mean and ignores true negatives. Accuracy can be high when a majority-class predictor ignores a rare class. The appropriate metric follows the decision cost, not a universal ranking.

In k-fold cross-validation, hold out one fold, fit the entire procedure on the others, score the held-out fold, and repeat. Imputation, scaling, feature selection and tuning must obey the same boundary. If tuning and reporting use the same folds, the best observed score is optimistically selected; nested resampling separates selection from outer evaluation.

Folds are not independent new datasets: their training subsets overlap. Repeating random seeds primarily measures aspects of procedure variability, not all uncertainty（不确定性） about the deployment population. Report sample sizes, class counts and the split structure alongside a mean score.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

A metric is a definition of which errors matter

Suppose only 2% of observations are positive. Predicting negative for everyone achieves 98% accuracy and detects no positive cases. Accuracy is not incorrect here; it answers a question that hides the rare-class failure. A confusion matrix（混淆矩阵） records true positives, false positives, false negatives and true negatives under a stated threshold and positive-class definition. Rates use different denominators: recall uses actual positives, while precision uses predicted positives.

Probability metrics retain information discarded by a threshold. Log loss penalizes confident mistakes; a Brier score averages squared probability errors. A ranking metric examines order rather than calibrated probability values. A model can rank（秩） cases well while systematically overestimating their probabilities. Decide whether the application needs ranking, probability estimation, decisions or several of these, then report matching metrics.

In regression, MSE（均方误差） emphasizes large errors, MAE is less sensitive to their magnitude, and R-squared compares residual（残差） sum of squares with variation around the evaluation-set mean. A negative held-out R-squared is possible. Specify units: MSE has squared target units, while RMSE returns to target units. A single convenient metric cannot replace the task definition.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

Cross-validation evaluates a fitting procedure

In k-fold cross-validation（交叉验证）, hold out each fold in turn and fit using the other folds. Fit preprocessing separately inside every training portion. The object evaluated is the full procedure: transformation, feature choices, hyperparameters and model fitting. Reusing a scaler or feature selector fitted on all observations lets information cross the fold boundary.

Searching many candidates and reporting the smallest cross-validation error uses those folds for selection. The selected minimum is typically optimistic as an estimate of that selected procedure's future error. An outer evaluation split or nested cross-validation（嵌套交叉验证） separates selection from assessment. Folds are not independent experiments simply because they have different numbers; their training sets overlap. Avoid presenting their standard deviation as a universal uncertainty guarantee.

详细讲解 · 第 1 学习日

The argument in mathematical notation

Prevalence changes precision

\mathrm{Precision}=\frac{\mathrm{TPR}\,\pi}{\mathrm{TPR}\,\pi+\mathrm{FPR}(1-\pi)}

Apply Bayes rule（贝叶斯公式） with positive prevalence pi. Holding conditional detection rates fixed does not hold precision fixed.

详细讲解 · 第 2 学习日

Compare calibration and discrimination with two explicit probability sets

Take labels [0,0,1,1] and predicted probabilities [0.1,0.2,0.8,0.9]. Their ranking separates classes perfectly. Now replace probabilities with [0.49,0.499,0.501,0.51]. Ranking remains perfect, and threshold 0.5 still gives the same class decisions, but the new predictions express almost no confidence. Log loss changes even though accuracy and ranking do not.

Conversely, an aggressive transformation can make a wrong prediction extremely confident without changing its position relative to other cases. Calibration asks whether events predicted around probability p occur about p of the time in a relevant population. Four observations are insufficient to establish calibration; the small example only separates concepts. Reliability diagrams need counts and sensible uncertainty treatment.

When your application only prioritizes a review queue, ranking may dominate the decision. When it estimates risk or chooses actions by cost, probability quality matters. State the use before choosing a headline metric. This is how the practical model output from the Handbook becomes the statistical evaluation object discussed by ISLP.

详细讲解 · 第 2 学习日

Compute metrics and a weighted validation mean

  1. Take TP=6, FP=2, FN=4, TN=88. There are 100 observations and 10 actual positives. Accuracy is 94/100=0.94.
  2. Precision is 6/8=0.75; recall is 6/10=0.6; F1 is 2*6/(2*6+2+4)=2/3. Each denominator reveals a different question.
  3. Now suppose validation folds have sizes 2 and 8, with mean losses 1 and 3. The mean over all held-out observations is (2*1+8*3)/10=2.6.
  4. The unweighted mean of fold means is 2. That assigns equal weight to folds rather than observations. Both quantities can be defined, but label the aggregation rule and use the one appropriate to the target population.
详细讲解 · 第 3 学习日

Keep threshold selection inside the development data

A threshold chosen to maximize validation F1 is a tuned parameter. Freeze it before final testing. Document behavior when a metric denominator is zero; for example, precision is undefined if no case is predicted positive. Some software reports zero by policy. Record that policy instead of treating a replacement value as a mathematical identity.

详细讲解 · 第 3 学习日

Calculate metrics with explicit denominators

tp, fp, fn, tn = 6, 2, 4, 88
precision = tp/(tp+fp)
recall = tp/(tp+fn)
f1 = 2*tp/(2*tp+fp+fn)
accuracy = (tp+tn)/(tp+fp+fn+tn)
weighted_loss = (2*1+8*3)/(2+8)
assert precision == 0.75
assert recall == 0.6
assert abs(f1-2/3) < 1e-12
assert accuracy == 0.94
assert weighted_loss == 2.6
print(precision, recall, f1, accuracy, weighted_loss)

详细讲解 · 第 4 学习日

Inspect how a conclusion changes with prevalence

Hold sensitivity and false-positive rate fixed in a thought experiment and change the positive-class prevalence. Precision changes because the mixture of actual positives and negatives changes. For example, a 10% false-positive rate can create many false alarms when negatives dominate. This explains why a classifier's reported precision does not automatically transfer to another setting.

Build an evaluation table with the baseline（基线）, one fixed model and one tuned model. Give the selection protocol, aggregation rule and actual sample counts. ISLP's resampling discussion supplies the statistical design; the Handbook and scikit-learn supply implementations. Their combination is useful only if the code follows the same boundaries the evaluation argument assumes.

详细讲解 · 第 5 学习日

Further practice with worked solutions

  1. Does excellent accuracy guarantee useful detection of a rare event?

    查看逐步解析

    No. An all-negative rule can have high accuracy when positives are rare. Inspect positive recall, false positives, precision and task costs alongside accuracy.

  2. Why fit an imputer separately in each fold?

    查看逐步解析

    The held-out fold represents unseen observations. Fitting imputation on it leaks distributional information into the fitted procedure and evaluates a different workflow from deployment.

Worked example

  1. Let TP=8, FP=2, FN=4 and TN=6. Accuracy is 14/20=0.7.
  2. Precision is 8/10=0.8; recall is 8/12=2/3; F1 is 16/22, approximately 0.7273.
  3. On a dataset with 99 negatives and 1 positive, always predicting negative gives 99% accuracy and zero positive recall.
  4. When no positives are predicted, precision has a zero denominator. State the software convention rather than treating that undefined ratio as a measured success.

Comparing the sources

ISLP Ch. 5 develops validation, cross-validation and bootstrap reasoning. CS229 §9 connects these choices to model selection. The Handbook shows search APIs; scikit-learn Pipeline turns the training boundary into an executable structure.

A bootstrap resamples observations with replacement; cross-validation trains and tests on different partitions. They serve related but different estimation purposes. Do not label the standard deviation of three training seeds as a bootstrap confidence interval.

Implementation

Test metric functions on explicit confusion counts and zero-denominator cases. State positive-class conventions.

Put learned preprocessing inside a Pipeline before cross-validation. Use grouped or temporal splits when rows are dependent.

Keep a final test untouched while selecting the metric, threshold and model settings. Save all candidates considered, not just the winner.

import numpy as np
from sklearn.datasets import load_breast_cancer
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split, StratifiedKFold, cross_val_score
from sklearn.pipeline import make_pipeline
from sklearn.preprocessing import StandardScaler
from sklearn.linear_model import LogisticRegression
from sklearn.metrics import confusion_matrix, precision_score, recall_score, f1_score

truth = np.array([1,1,1,0,0]); pred = np.array([1,0,1,1,0])
print("TN FP / FN TP:\n", confusion_matrix(truth,pred,labels=[0,1]))
print("P R F1:", precision_score(truth,pred), recall_score(truth,pred), f1_score(truth,pred))
X, y = load_breast_cancer(return_X_y=True)
Xdev, Xtest, ydev, ytest = train_test_split(X,y,test_size=.2,stratify=y,random_state=42)
pipeline = make_pipeline(StandardScaler(), LogisticRegression(max_iter=1000))
cv = StratifiedKFold(n_splits=3,shuffle=True,random_state=42)
scores = cross_val_score(pipeline,Xdev,ydev,cv=cv,scoring="f1")
print("development CV F1:",scores, "mean:",scores.mean())
# A teaching dataset, not medical advice or a clinically validated model.
# Test data are reserved; no model selection on Xtest/ytest.

One-page recap

The evaluation procedure is part of the model. Protect its boundaries as carefully as the derivative code.

precision（精确率）: The fraction of predicted positives that are true positives.

recall（召回率）: The fraction of actual positives that are predicted positive.

cross-validation（交叉验证）: Repeated fitting and validation across held-out folds.

selection bias（选择偏差）: Distortion introduced by choosing or reporting results based on their observed values.
