Readiness check（起点筛查）

You already know basic Python, calculus and probability. Treat these as starting assets. Use the checks to locate a specific gap; a missed matrix question does not mean restarting Python.

A worked connection

Work without running code for 15 minutes. Then use 15 minutes to inspect the solutions and 30 minutes to repair the weakest topic. Skip the old readiness chapter if the programming checks are comfortable.

  1. What does a function that only prints 3 return?

    查看解析

  2. For X with shape (4,3), what shape does X @ w have when w is (3,)?

    查看解析

  3. Differentiate (wx+b-y)^2 with respect to w.

    2(wx+b-y)x, holding x, b and y fixed.

  4. If P(A)=0.2 and P(B|A)=0.5, what is P(A and B)?

    查看解析

    0.1, by the product rule. P(A|B) also needs P(B).

  5. May a scaler use the held-out data mean?

    查看解析

    Not when evaluating a procedure that must fit preprocessing on training data only.

From vectors to projection（从向量到投影）

A matrix is both a table and a linear map（线性映射）. Let X have two rows [1,0] and [1,1] and let w=[2,3]. Multiplication gives predictions [2,5]. Columns describe how changing a parameter changes all predictions: the first shifts both observations, while the second changes only the second. This is why column space（列空间） describes all predictions available to a linear model.

A worked connection

Project y=[2,0] onto v=[1,1]. The scalar coefficient is (v dot y)/(v dot v)=2/2=1. The projection is [1,1], and the residual is [1,-1]. Their dot product is zero. To see why this coefficient minimizes squared error, expand ||y-av||^2=4-4a+2a^2 and differentiate: -4+4a=0 gives a=1. Your existing calculus has produced a geometric least-squares solution. With redundant columns, coefficients need not be unique even when the fitted prediction is unique. Use a numerical least-squares solver rather than an explicit inverse.

  1. For X of shape (5,2) and w of shape (2,), what does each prediction represent?

    查看解析

    One predicted value per row, giving shape (5,).

  2. Why is the residual perpendicular to v at the optimum?

    查看解析

    Changing the coefficient along v has zero first derivative: v dot (av-y)=0.

  3. What changes when both columns are identical?

    查看解析

    Multiple coefficient pairs can give the same prediction; coefficient identification fails.

From one derivative to reverse mode（从导数到反向传播）

A partial derivative（偏导数） changes one input coordinate while fixing the others. A gradient（梯度） collects these derivatives for a scalar objective. It is not an arbitrary new differentiation rule: apply your existing chain rule one coordinate at a time, then collect the result in the same shape as the parameters.

A worked connection

Take x=[1,2], w=[1,0], b=0 and y=3. The score z=w dot x+b=1, the residual r=-2, and squared loss L=4. dL/dz=2r=-4. Multiplying by dz/dw gives gradient [-4,-8], and dL/db=-4. A learning rate 0.01 changes w to [1.04,0.08] and b to 0.04; the new score is 1.24 and loss is 3.0976. For n cases with mean loss, divide the accumulated gradient by n. If one value contributes through two graph paths, add both derivative contributions. The same rule powers backpropagation（反向传播）.

  1. Why does the gradient of w have shape (2,) here?

    查看解析

    There is one partial derivative for each of the two coordinates of w.

  2. What is the central difference for the first coordinate with h=0.001?

    查看解析

    [L(w+[h,0])-L(w-[h,0])]/(2h)=-4 for this quadratic in exact arithmetic.

  3. When is the negative gradient guaranteed to improve any finite step?

    查看解析

    There is no such universal finite-step guarantee; a sufficiently small step at a smooth nonstationary point gives local decrease.

From probability to statistical learning（从概率到统计学习）

Probability fixes a model and varies outcomes. Likelihood（似然） fixes the observed outcomes and compares parameter values. For independent Bernoulli observations [1,1,0], the likelihood of p is p^2(1-p). Maximizing it gives p=2/3. Taking a logarithm turns products into sums without changing the maximizer. Negative average log likelihood is the binary cross-entropy loss（交叉熵损失） for constant predictions.

A worked connection

The empirical risk（经验风险） is a sample average, not a guarantee about future cases. Fit parameters on training data; use validation data to choose a threshold or penalty; use the test set after those decisions are frozen. Conditional probability also explains precision: with prevalence 0.1, true-positive rate 0.8 and false-positive rate 0.1, precision is 0.08/(0.08+0.09), about 0.471. A high detection rate alone does not describe the false alarms users see. Your probability knowledge becomes useful when the population, conditioning event and denominator are explicit.

  1. Differentiate the log likelihood 2 log(p)+log(1-p).

    查看解析

    2/p-1/(1-p)=0 gives p=2/3 in (0,1).

  2. Does a chosen threshold count as fitting?

    查看解析

    It is a model-selection decision and must stay inside development data.

  3. Why does changing prevalence change precision?

    查看解析

    The counts of real positives and false alarms change relative to each other, even if conditional detection rates stay fixed.
